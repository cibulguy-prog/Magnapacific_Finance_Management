Income sheet — click any cell to edit, just like a spreadsheet

Payments tracking — record every payment per client and attach its receipt

Payments log

One row per payment received. Pick the booking it belongs to, then upload the receipt (image or PDF, up to 8 MB). Receipts are stored privately in your Google Drive and linked here — only you can open them while signed in to Google.

Expenses sheet — click any cell to edit, just like a spreadsheet

Fixed monthly expenses — entered automatically every month

Expenses that repeat every month (mortgage, internet, salaries…). Once a row is active, a matching expense is added to the sheet below automatically for every month from From month up to the current month — and a new one each month after that. A month that already has a row for the same type, group and category is never duplicated. Delete an auto-added row and it will not come back.

Currency: every record keeps its original currency (USD / ILS) in the sheets. The overview converts everything to USD using the exchange rate shown in the "How the total is built" panel — change it there and all totals follow.
Income: Gross is the price of the stay, Net price is the gross after the platform / agency commission. A stay counts in the year and month of its check-in. Influencer stays count nights but no income. Payments are tracked against the net price.
Channels: Direct, Airbnb, and Agencies (every other platform or agent). A stay sold as "airbnb + direct" is counted under Airbnb — change its platform if you want it elsewhere.
Historical data: the stays were loaded from your Excel file (dates from its "date of stay" column and year). Payment dates were not in the file, so they start out equal to the check-in date — overwrite them as you go.
